Synthetic enterprise evidence — Knowledge Product Manager demonstration data
Effective: 2026-01-01. Status: Active. Applies to Treasury Liquidity and FIN positions.
## Controls
Treasury must reconcile liquidity positions daily. Each position requires an as-of date, currency and reconciled funding source. Liquidity for FIN-100 is USD 12,500,000 as of 2026-09-30; this illustrative value must be checked against the position dataset.
## Escalation
A liquidity buffer below USD 10,000,000 requires same-day escalation to the Treasury sponsor.
